# Focus Bear Product Overview

## Things relevant to my role as a Windows developer

1. Focus Bear supports the full focus workflow rather than only blocking distractions. Users define an intention, configure blocking, complete a timed focus session, review statistics, reflect on their work, and decide what to do next.

2. Focus sessions combine several systems, including Focus Modes, Block Lists, AI-assisted website decisions, strictness levels, timers, breaks, and password protection. Changes to one part of the application may therefore affect several related workflows.

3. The focus goal has a functional purpose. In addition to reminding the user what to work on, it may be used by the AI feature to decide whether websites are relevant to the task.

4. Focus Bear intentionally adds friction through delayed stopping, strict sessions, time locking, and Partner Password. This helps users avoid impulsively disabling blocking.

5. Features such as the brain dump, Smart To-Do List, focus music, session extension, statistics, and reflection support concentration without forcing users to ignore unrelated thoughts or tasks.

## Reflection

### What did I learn that I didn't know before?

Before reading the documentation and watching the onboarding videos, I thought Focus Bear was mainly an app and website blocker. I learned that it is a complete productivity platform that guides users through the entire focus process—from setting an intention, blocking distractions, and managing breaks, to reviewing progress and reflecting after each session.

I also learned that Focus Bear uses friction intentionally. Different strictness levels, passwords, and AI-assisted blocking help users stay committed to their goals instead of impulsively disabling focus sessions.

---

### Documentation improvements

While reviewing the documentation and onboarding videos, I noticed a few areas that may benefit from review.

- Different names are used for strictness levels (Normal/Strict/Super Strict versus Chill/Wait for It/Iron Focus).

- One video mentions a 15-second delay before stopping a session, while another article mentions a 15-minute delay for Wait for It mode. These may refer to different features but could be clarified.

- The relationship between Focus Modes, Block Lists, AI Blocking, Cuddly Bear Mode, Grizzly Bear Mode, and Blocking Schedule could be explained more clearly for new users.

- The desktop and mobile documentation use different navigation paths and screenshots. Adding clearer platform labels or updating screenshots would make the documentation easier to follow.
